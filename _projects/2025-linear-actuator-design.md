For statics we were asked to sketch a design of a linear actuator mechanism given three pins, a long beam of our chosen length and a linear actuator picked from a catalog. For my design, I wanted to multiply the maximum force provided by the linear actuator to lift a mass to some max height. I decided to go with a cantilever design where the mass is at one end of the beam and the linear actuator is at the other end. The pin rests at 2/3 the length of the beam from the left. This design allows the linear actuator to lift a max weight of 4kN to 1/5 the length of the beam, effectively multiplying the max force output of 2kN by a factor of 2.
