<div class="home-section">
  <h2>About me</h2>
  <p>I am currently pursuing a degree in Mechanical Engineering within the Cornell University College of Engineering, inspired by my four-year experience on my high school’s FRC Robotics team. Through robotics, I developed foundational skills in programming, electronics, manufacturing, and design, as well as communication, teamwork, and leadership.</p>

  <p>In my senior year, I led our team to qualify for the FRC World Championship for the first time in the 21-year history of the program. I mentored underclassmen, teaching them to break down complex problems and collaborate to design innovative solutions. I also introduced them to key principles of design and manufacturing, leveraging my experience with CAD and CAM software developed over my four years on the team.</p>

  <p>My technical expertise includes four years of CAD experience with Fusion 360, four years of manufacturing experience with a 3-axis CNC machine using Fusion 360’s built-in CAM tools, and two years of programming experience in Java.</p>

  <p>Beyond robotics, I honed my leadership skills through an internship with CreateAccess, a non-profit startup focused on 3D education for underserved communities. In this role, I led in-person learning demonstrations for high school students and helped foster hands-on innovation.</p>

  <p>I am also passionate about entrepreneurship and sustainability, and I aspire to integrate societal impact and growth into my engineering work. I pursued these interests at the Iacocca Global Entrepreneurship Intensive at Lehigh University, where I spent a month working with local companies and studying entrepreneurship in a hands-on environment.</p>
